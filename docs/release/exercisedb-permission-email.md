**Subject:** Commercial use of ExerciseDB exercise animations in the FitNFree app

Hello,

I'm Salix Kamara of LixTeam, and I publish FitNFree, a fitness and health app for iPhone and Android (bundle ID com.teamk.fitnfree). It's live on the Apple App Store and in testing on Google Play.

FitNFree shows exercise animations on its exercise detail screens. Currently about 100 of them are loaded directly from `static.exercisedb.dev`. The app has a free tier and an optional paid Premium subscription.

Before I go further, I'd like to be sure I'm using your material properly. Could you tell me:

1. Do I have your permission to show these animations in a commercial app, including one with a paid subscription?
2. If so, under what licence or terms, and is there anything in writing I can keep for my records (an email reply is fine)?
3. Is loading the files directly from your servers acceptable, or do you prefer that I download and host copies myself?
4. Do you require attribution, and if so, how should it be worded and where should it appear?
5. Is there a paid plan or commercial licence, and what does it cost?

If commercial use isn't permitted, please let me know and I'll remove the animations.

Thank you for your time and for the work on the project.

Kind regards,
Salix Kamara
LixTeam
teamlix6@gmail.com

## Notes

* Keep any reply. A written yes is your proof of rights for the Content Rights question in App Store Connect.
* A "no", or no answer, does not change the safe fallback: remove the GIFs in a new build (the app falls back to text).
* Do not tick the "I have the rights" confirmation for ExerciseDB until you have a written yes.
* Add the FitNFree App Store link to the email if you want it to look more established.
