# FitNFree 1.0.1 tester checklist

Thank you for testing. This takes about 20 minutes. Tick each line, and note anything that fails, crashes, freezes or looks wrong.

**Build:** iPhone: TestFlight build 19. Android: update from the Play testing link you were sent (version 1.0.1).
**Before you start:** if you had FitNFree 1.0 installed, keep it and update over it. Do not delete it first.

## Everyone (iPhone and Android)

- [ ] **Old data survived.** If you used 1.0, your login, past workouts and settings are all still there.
- [ ] **Sign in and out.** Sign out, then sign in again.
- [ ] **Body tab.** Log a weight and a measurement. Check the trend chart. Add a progress photo (it stays on your phone).
- [ ] **Log a workout.** Beat a previous set. A personal record badge should appear. Then open Progress > Records.
- [ ] **Reminders.** Set a workout reminder for a few minutes ahead. Does it arrive?
- [ ] **Rest timer.** Run it after a set.
- [ ] **Interval Clock.** Run a Tabata or boxing round. Check the sound and vibration.
- [ ] **Export my data.** In Profile, export JSON and CSV, then open the files.
- [ ] **Wearables screen.** It should say "Connect on the web app" and show no "Coming soon" list.
- [ ] **Shop.** Free guides open and read properly.
- [ ] **Premium (iPhone).** Open the paywall: price, renewal terms and Restore Purchases show. Do not buy.
- [ ] **Delete My Account.** Use a throwaway account only, never your real one.

## Two or more people together

- [ ] **Friends.** Turn it on, send a request, accept it, send a cheer. Does it arrive?
- [ ] **Report and block** a friend (use a spare account).
- [ ] **Coaching check-in.** One person is the trainer and gives a code. The other uses it, sends a check-in, and reads the reply. The trainer can press **Report** on the client.

## iPhone only

- [ ] **Home screen widget.** Add the streak widget (iOS 17 or later). The streak updates after a workout.
- [ ] **Apple Watch.** Install it. Does your streak show? Can you log today's workout?
- [ ] **iPad.** Open the app and look for cut-off or overlapping screens.

## How to report a problem

Email **teamlix6@gmail.com** with: your phone model, iPhone or Android, which step failed, what you expected, and a screenshot if you can. A crash or freeze is the most important thing to tell us.
